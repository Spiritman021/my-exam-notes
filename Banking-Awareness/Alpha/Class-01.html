2Financial Inclusion

The first “Important Term” of the batch.

🏦 BankingFinancial Inclusionवित्तीय समावेशन

  • Financial inclusion is the effort to make financial products and services available and affordable to all individuals and businesses.
➕ Added for exam
  • Formal definition (Rangarajan Committee, 2008): ensuring access to financial services and timely, adequate credit for vulnerable groups such as weaker sections and low-income groups, at an affordable cost.
  • Flagship scheme: PMJDYPMJDYPradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan YojanaLaunched 28 Aug 2014National Mission for Financial InclusionZero-balance account + RuPay debit card (launched 28 August 2014) – one basic bank account for every household. Taught in Beta Class 9.
  • RBIRBIReserve Bank of IndiaEst. 1 April 1935 (RBI Act 1934)Nationalised 1949HQ Mumbai measures it with the Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index), scored 0–100 on three parts: Access 35%, Usage 45%, Quality 20%. Taught in Beta Class 1.

3Business Correspondents (BC)

The second “Important Term”: how banks reach villages that have no branch.

🏦 BankingBusiness Correspondentsव्यवसाय संवाददाता

  • Business Correspondents are retail agents engaged by banks for providing banking services at locations other than a bank branch / ATM.
  • BCs let a bank expand its outreach and offer a limited range of banking services at low cost.
  • Why: setting up a brick-and-mortar branch may not be viable in all cases.
➕ Added for exam
  • RBIRBIReserve Bank of IndiaEst. 1 April 1935 (RBI Act 1934)Nationalised 1949HQ Mumbai allowed banks to use the BC (and Business Facilitator) model in January 2006.
  • BCs are popularly called Bank Mitra. They work with a hand-held device or micro-ATM, often using Aadhaar-based payments (AePS).
  • Typical BC services: opening accounts, small deposits and withdrawals, remittances, balance enquiry, and collecting loan documents.
⚠️ Exam trapBC vs BF: a Business Correspondent can do cash transactions on behalf of the bank. A Business Facilitator only refers or helps customers (spreading awareness, collecting applications) and does not handle cash.

Branch vs BC – the picture on slide 17

  • Left picture: the old brick-and-mortar branch, costly to open in every village.
  • Right picture: banking reaching people through agents and phones, which is the BC idea.

4Assets and Liability

Two accounting words you need before the banking-system classes.

What is an 'Asset'?संपत्ति / परिसंपत्ति

  • Assets are things you own that you can sell for money.
  • In accounting, an asset is any resource that a business owns or controls.
  • It's anything that could be sold for money.

What is 'Liability'?देनदारी / दायित्व

  • A liability is an obligation to pay money to other people or businesses in the future.
  • Liability usually means you are responsible for something. It can also mean you owe someone money or services.
➕ Added for exam
  • Balance-sheet rule: Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s equity (capital).
⚠️ Exam trapThink from the bank’s side: your deposit is money the bank owes you, so it is the bank’s liability. The loan the bank gives you will be repaid to it, so it is the bank’s asset. Exams love this reversal.

Name the three parts of General Awareness.

Current Affairs, Banking / Financial Awareness and Static GK.

Define financial inclusion.

The effort to make financial products and services available and affordable to all individuals and businesses.

What is a Business Correspondent?

A retail agent engaged by a bank to provide banking services at locations other than a bank branch or ATM.

Why do banks use BCs instead of opening branches everywhere?

BCs expand outreach and give limited services at low cost; a brick-and-mortar branch may not be viable everywhere.

Which of these can do cash transactions for a bank – Business Correspondent or Business Facilitator?

Business Correspondent. A Business Facilitator only refers / assists customers.

For a bank, is a customer’s deposit an asset or a liability?

A liability (the bank owes it back to the customer).

For a bank, is a loan given to a customer an asset or a liability?

An asset.

What is an asset in accounting?

Any resource that a business owns or controls; anything that could be sold for money.
