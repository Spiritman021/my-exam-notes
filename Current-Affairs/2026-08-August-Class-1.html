🏦 BankingHDFC BankHDFC BankIncorporated 1994HQ MumbaiMerged with parent HDFC Ltd on 1 July 2023Tagline "We understand your world" Launches New Savings Accounts for Senior Citizens and Women

  • HDFC Bank has introduced two specialised savings-account variants for specific customer groups: Specialé Senior Citizen Savings Account and SavingsMax Account for Women.
  • Specialé Senior Citizen Savings Account — focuses on security, healthcare and convenient banking services for senior citizens.
  • SavingsMax Account for Women — a premium savings account offering a combination of banking, health, insurance and lifestyle benefits.

🏦 BankingKarnataka BankKarnataka BankFounded 18 February 1924HQ Mangaluru, KarnatakaPrivate-sector bank launches ‘KBL Finsurance’ platform

  • Karnataka Bank has launched ‘KBL Finsurance’, a comprehensive digital portal designed to streamline the creation, tracking and monitoring of insurance business leads across its branch network.
  • The portal has been developed with SprintMoney, a fintech platform operated by Wildflower Business Services Private Limited.
  • It will connect Karnataka Bank with its partner insurance companies through a single digital platform.
⚠️ Exam trapDon't mix up the partners: the portal is KBL Finsurance, built with fintech SprintMoney, which is run by Wildflower Business Services.

🏦 BankingShivalik Small Finance Bank Acquires ManiBhavnam Home Finance

  • Shivalik Small Finance Bank has completed the acquisition and merger of Delhi-based ManiBhavnam Home Finance India Private Limited.
  • The transaction was completed at a value of nearly ₹109 crore.
  • Shivalik Small Finance Bank: began as Shivalik Mercantile Co-operative Bank; became India’s first Urban Co-operative Bank to transition into a Small Finance Bank; commenced operations as an SFB in April 2021; Headquarters: New Delhi.
➕ Static GK: Small Finance Banks — Licensed by RBI · Min. paid-up capital ₹200 crore (on-tap guidelines, 2019) · 75% of ANBC to priority sector · 50% of loans must be up to ₹25 lakh
⚠️ Exam trapShivalik is the first Urban Co-operative Bank to become an SFB, not the first SFB overall. It became an SFB in April 2021.

3Economy, GST & Payments

🎯 Likely QGross GSTGSTGoods and Services TaxIn force from 1 July 2017101st Constitutional Amendment collection was ₹2.11 lakh crore in July

  • The gross collection from Goods & Services Tax (GST) surged over 15.4 per cent to over ₹2.11 lakh crore in July.
  • GST was officially launched on 1st July 2017.
  • GST was introduced through the Constitution (101st Amendment) Act, 2016.
  • Following the recommendations of the 56th GST CouncilGST CouncilConstitutional body under Article 279AChaired by the Union Finance MinisterGST replaced multiple indirect taxes with one tax from 1 July 2017 meeting, the earlier four major slabs of 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% were rationalised.
  • From 22 September 2025, GST primarily has: Nil — exempt or essential supplies · 5% — merit rate for essential and mass-consumption items · 18% — standard rate for most goods and services · 40% — special demerit rate for selected luxury and sin goods.
  • GSTIN stands for Goods and Services Tax Identification Number; it is a 15-digit identification number issued to registered taxpayers.
⚠️ Exam trapGST came through the 101st Amendment Act, 2016 (the Bill was numbered the 122nd Amendment Bill). After 22 Sept 2025 the 12% and 28% slabs are gone; the new slabs are Nil, 5%, 18% and 40%.

🎯 Likely QUPIUPIUnified Payments InterfaceLaunched 2016 by NPCI Records Highest-Ever Monthly Volume in July 2026

  • India’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) processed a record 23.66 billion transactions, or 2,366 crore transactions, in July 2026.
  • The total value of transactions stood at ₹29.88 lakh crore.
⚠️ Exam trapSame number, two units: 23.66 billion = 2,366 crore transactions. The value is ₹29.88 lakh crore. Don't mix it with July GST (₹2.11 lakh crore).

🎯 Likely QCentre releases additional ₹1.09 lakh crore in tax devolution to states

  • Union Government released an additional instalment of ₹1,09,019 crore as tax devolution to State governments on 1 August 2026.
  • This amount is in addition to the normal monthly tax devolution scheduled to be released on 10 August 2026.
  • Uttar Pradesh received the largest share at ₹19,208 crore, followed by Bihar at ₹10,845 crore and Madhya Pradesh at ₹8,010 crore.
  • What is Tax Devolution? The transfer of a prescribed share of the Centre’s divisible pool of taxes to State governments — the States’ constitutionally determined share in certain taxes collected by the Union Government.
  • Union Government has accepted the 16th Finance CommissionFinance CommissionConstitutional body under Article 280Constituted every 5 years by the President16th FC chaired by Arvind Panagariya (award period 2026-27 to 2030-31)15th FC chaired by N.K. Singh’s recommendation to retain the States’ vertical share of tax devolution at 41% of the divisible pool.
  • The amount is distributed among States through 14 instalments during a financial year.
⚠️ Exam trapThe States' share was retained at 41% (same as the 15th FC), not raised. It is paid in 14 instalments a year. Top three: UP > Bihar > MP.

🎯 Likely QGovt offload 6.54% in LICLICEst. 1 September 1956HQ MumbaiListed on the stock exchanges in May 2022 (India's largest IPO at the time)Normal Minimum Public Shareholding for listed companies is 25%

  • Government of India announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) to reduce its shareholding in the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).
  • Under the main OFS, the government sold up to 6.5% equity — Base offer: 2.5% · Green shoe option: additional 4% if investor demand is strong.
  • The OFS helped LIC meet the Minimum Public Shareholding requirement.
  • Present public shareholding: 3.5% · Public shareholding after full 6.5% OFS: 10% · Deadline to achieve 10% public shareholding: 16 May 2027.
⚠️ Exam trapThe headline says 6.54%, the details say up to 6.5% (2.5% base + 4% green shoe). Public shareholding goes 3.5% → 10%; deadline 16 May 2027.

5Union Cabinet Approvals

🎯 Likely QUnion Cabinet Approves PM Surya Sarovar Yojana

  • Union Cabinet has approved the Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana (PM-SSY) to develop large-scale Floating Solar Photovoltaic projects integrated with Energy Storage Systems.
  • The scheme has a total financial outlay of ₹5,070 crore and will be implemented over five years.
  • It targets the development of 5,000 MW — or 5 GW — of floating solar capacity across India.
⚠️ Exam trapPM Surya Sarovar = floating solar, ₹5,070 crore, 5 GW. PM Surya Ghar = rooftop solar for 1 crore households (World Bank $890 mn, July Class 6).

🎯 Likely QUnion Cabinet gives nod to continuation of PM-KISANPM-KISANLaunched 24 February 2019 at Gorakhpur, UPCentral Sector Scheme (100% Centre-funded)Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare Scheme for next five years

  • Union Cabinet has approved the continuation of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Scheme from 2026-27 to 2030-31.
  • Financial Outlay: ₹3,15,614 crore.
  • Since its launch in 2019, more than ₹4.47 lakh crore has been transferred through 23 instalments under the scheme.
  • PM-KISAN provides ₹6,000 annually to eligible landholding farmer families through three equal instalments deposited directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
⚠️ Exam trap₹6,000 a year is paid as three instalments of ₹2,000 each, by DBT. 23 instalments released so far.

🎯 Likely QUnion Cabinet Approves ₹84,084 Crore ‘Samudra Manthan’ Scheme

  • Union Cabinet has approved the National Offshore Exploration Scheme — Samudra Manthan, with a total outlay of ₹84,084 crore for implementation up to FY 2030–31.
  • The scheme seeks to accelerate the exploration of oil and natural-gas resources in India’s offshore basins, particularly in deepwater and ultra-deepwater regions.
  • It is being implemented under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.
➕ Static GK: Directorate General of Hydrocarbons — Upstream oil & gas regulator · Est. 1993 · Under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
⚠️ Exam trapSamudra Manthan (offshore oil & gas, MoPNG) is different from the Deep Ocean Mission / Samudrayaan (Ministry of Earth Sciences, Matsya-6000 submersible).
