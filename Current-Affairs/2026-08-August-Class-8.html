3Markets, Disinvestment & Regulators

🎯 Likely QSEBISEBISecurities and Exchange Board of IndiaFormed 12 April 1988Statutory 30 January 1992HQ Mumbai Launches IT Resilience Index (ITRI) for MIIs

  • SEBI introduced the Information Technology Resilience Index (ITRI) for Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs): stock exchanges, depositories and clearing corporations.
  • Computed on nine parameters: Availability 20%, Security 20%; Integrity, Governance, Reliability & Monitoring, Business Continuity, Modularity & Flexibility 10% each; Scalability and Incident Handling 5% each.
  • MIIs must also build an Early Warning System (EWS) to spot IT deterioration before slowdowns or outages.
  • Fully operational by 28 February 2027; first computation for the half-year ending 31 March 2027; then half-yearly, within 60 days.
⚠️ Exam trapHighest weights: Availability and Security (20% each). Lowest: Scalability and Incident Handling (5% each).
