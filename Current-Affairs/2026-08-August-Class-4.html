1Banking, Payments & Insurance

🏦 BankingUPIUPILaunched 2016Operated by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India, est. 2008, HQ Mumbai)MDR = the fee a merchant pays its bank for accepting digital payments Payments to Remain Free for Users

  • Central Government clarified that users will not be charged for UPI payments, amid concerns over the proposed amendment to the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.
  • All Person-to-Person (P2P) UPI transactions will remain completely free; no charge on normal everyday UPI transactions by citizens.
  • If a Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) is introduced in future, it will apply only to a limited category of merchant transactions, only above a specified threshold, at a nominal rate, and well below the MDR on debit and credit cards.
  • The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 proposes to amend Section 10A of the PSS Act, 2007.
⚠️ Exam trapMDR is paid by the merchant, never by the customer. The amendment is to Section 10A of the PSS Act, 2007, through the Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026.

  • Estimated deal value: around ₹3.25 lakh crore.

5Important Days & Anniversaries

🎯 Likely QSeventh Anniversary of Abrogation of Article 370 Observed on 5 August

  • India marks seven years since the provisions of Article 370, which gave special status to the erstwhile State of Jammu and Kashmir, were made inoperative in August 2019.
  • The process began on 5 August 2019 (Presidential Order and resolution introduced in Parliament); Parliament completed approval on 6 August 2019.
  • So 5 August is generally observed as the anniversary.
➕ Static GK: J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 — Created two UTs, Jammu & Kashmir (with legislature) and Ladakh (without), from 31 October 2019 · Supreme Court upheld the abrogation on 11 December 2023
⚠️ Exam trapIntroduced 5 Aug 2019, approved 6 Aug 2019; the UTs came into being on 31 Oct 2019. 2026 = 7th anniversary.

World Marks 81 Years since US Dropped Atomic Bomb on Japan

  • Hiroshima Day is observed on 6 August for the 1945 atomic bombing of Hiroshima.
  • On 6 and 9 August 1945 the US detonated two atomic bombs over Hiroshima and Nagasaki during World War II.
  • “Little Boy” — enriched uranium, gun-type; “Fat Man” — plutonium, implosion-type.
  • A US B-29 bomber dropped “Little Boy” on 6 August 1945.
➕ Static GK: Bombers — “Little Boy” was dropped by the B-29 Enola Gay (Hiroshima); “Fat Man” by Bockscar (Nagasaki)
⚠️ Exam trapLittle Boy = Uranium = Hiroshima (6 Aug). Fat Man = Plutonium (Pu) = Nagasaki (9 Aug).

Nagasaki Marks 81st Anniversary of Atomic Bombing

  • Nagasaki marked the 81st anniversary of the US atomic bombing on 9 August 2026.
  • The annual Peace Memorial Ceremony was held at the Peace Park in Nagasaki.
  • The bombing came three days after Hiroshima (6 August 1945).
  • Japan announced its surrender on 15 August 1945, ending World War II in Asia.

International Day of Awareness of the Special Development Needs and Challenges of Landlocked Developing Countries — 6 August

  • The United Nations observes 6 August every year for the special needs and challenges of Landlocked Developing Countries (LLDCsLLDCsThe UN group has 32 landlocked developing countriesIndia’s landlocked neighbours: Nepal, Bhutan (also Afghanistan)).
  • A landlocked country has no direct coastline or access to an ocean or sea.
⚠️ Exam trapSame date as Hiroshima Day (6 Aug) — don’t mix them up.

🎯 Likely QIndia Observes 12th National Handloom Day

  • National Handloom Day is celebrated every year on 7 August, since 2015, to honour handloom weavers and textile traditions.
  • 2026 marks the 12th National Handloom Day.
  • On 7 August 1905 the Swadeshi Movement was formally launched at Calcutta Town Hall against the Partition of Bengal.
➕ Static GK: First National Handloom Day — Inaugurated by PM Narendra Modi in Chennai, 2015 · Ministry of Textiles
⚠️ Exam trapDate chosen to mark the Swadeshi Movement (7 Aug 1905). 2015 = 1st, so 2026 = 12th.

🎯 Likely QNational Javelin Day Is Observed Every Year on 7 August

  • Commemorates Neeraj ChopraNeeraj ChopraTokyo gold throw 87.58 m on 7 August 2021India’s first Olympic gold in athletics’s gold in the men’s javelin throw at the Tokyo Olympics 2020, and promotes the sport.
  • 2026 marks the 5th National Javelin Day.

International Day of the World’s Indigenous Peoples Is Observed on 9 August

  • Observed every year on 9 August to raise awareness about the rights, cultures and challenges of Indigenous Peoples.
  • Theme 2026: “Honouring Indigenous Midwives: Safeguarding Life and Well-being”.
➕ Static GK: Origin — Declared by the UN General Assembly in 1994; the date marks the first meeting of the UN Working Group on Indigenous Populations (1982)
