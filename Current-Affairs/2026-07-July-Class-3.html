2Defence & Security

Army · Navy · Coast Guard · industry — one item each, plus RIMPAC.

🎯 Likely QArmy Chief unveils 'VIJAY' roadmap

  • Unveiled by COAS General Dhiraj Seth; inspired by MoD's "Decade of Transformation".
  • VIJAY = Vigilance · Innovation & Transformation · Jointness & Integration · Atmanirbharta · Yodha First.
⚠️ Exam trapThe acronym is the question: V-I-J-A-Y → Vigilance, Innovation, Jointness, Atmanirbharta, Yodha First.

Indian Coast Guard commissions ICGS Akshay in Goa

  • Indigenous Fast Patrol Vessel; 4th of the 8-ship Adamya-class.
  • Built by Goa Shipyard Ltd.

🎯 Likely QIndia at RIMPAC 2026, Hawaii

  • 30th edition of Rim of the Pacific — world's largest multinational maritime exercise; biennial.
  • Hosted by the US Navy (U.S. Pacific Fleet) at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam; 31 nations.
  • Theme: "Partners: Integrated and Prepared".

INS Udaygiri, Shakti and Kavaratti reach Changi Naval Base, Singapore

  • Eastern Fleet deployment to South East Asia, led by Rear Admiral Alok Ananda.
  • 2026 is the ASEAN-India Year of Maritime Cooperation.

Adani Group's ₹2,500 crore defence plant at Shivpuri, MP

  • At Pali village, Shivpuri district on NH-27, Madhya Pradesh; investment ~₹2,500 crore.
  • Foundation stone by Jyotiraditya Scindia and MP CM Dr. Mohan Yadav.

4Science & Technology

🎯 Likely QSkyroot unveils Vikram-1 — India's first private orbital rocket

  • By Skyroot Aerospace; named after Dr. Vikram Sarabhai, father of India's space programme.
  • Can place up to 350 kg in Low Earth Orbit.

Mission ShaktiSAT — all-girls Moon satellite mission

  • World's first all-girls international satellite mission to the Moon; led by Space Kidz India.
  • 12,000 girls from 108 countries.
  • Gautam Buddha University joins as academic partner, with IN-SPACeIN-SPACeIndian National Space Promotion and Authorization CentreEst. 2020HQ AhmedabadAuthorises private space activity; GNIDA also supports.

Ministry of Tourism + GoogleGoogleFounded 4 September 1998HQ California, USACEO Sundar Pichai India MoU

  • Uses AI and data to promote Indian tourism; Ministry's first comprehensive tie-up with a global tech company.
  • Signed in presence of Tourism Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat.
