2Economy & Business

🎯 Likely QPM dedicates India's 24th refinery at Pachpadra, Rajasthan

  • Location: Pachpadra, Balotra district, Rajasthan; India's 24th refinery.
  • India's first Greenfield Integrated Refinery-cum-Petrochemical Complex.
  • Built by HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Ltd (HRRL): HPCL 74%, Rajasthan govt 26%.
  • Investment ₹79,450 crore+; refining 9 MMTPA; petrochemicals 2.4 MMTPA.

🎯 Likely QCG Semi's ₹7,500 crore OSAT facility opens at Sanand, Gujarat

  • OSAT = Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test; marks start of commercial chip production.
  • Partners: Renesas Electronics (Japan) and Stars Microelectronics (Thailand).
  • Among the first four projects approved under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM).
➕ Static GK: Gujarat chip map: Tata Electronics fab — Dholera · Micron ATMP — Sanand · CG Semi OSAT — Sanand · Kaynes OSAT.
⚠️ Exam trapDholera = Tata's fab; Sanand = Micron and CG Semi assembly/test plants. Don't mix them up.
