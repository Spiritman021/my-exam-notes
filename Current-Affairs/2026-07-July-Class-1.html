🎯 Likely QVB-G RAM G Act in force — ₹300 daily floor wage

  • Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 in force from 1 July 2026.
  • Replaces MGNREGA, 2005; guarantees 125 days of wage employment per rural household per year.
  • National floor wage: ₹300/day; average notified wage ₹298.8 → ₹327.4.
  • Highest % hike: Arunachal Pradesh and Nagaland (~24.5%).
  • Above ₹400/day: Haryana ₹409, Goa ₹406, Kerala ₹401, Sikkim high-altitude GPs ₹450.
⚠️ Exam trapMGNREGA guaranteed 100 days; the new Act says 125 days.
