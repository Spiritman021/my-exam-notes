4Government, Schemes & Policy

🎯 Likely QEPFOEPFOEmployees’ Provident Fund OrganisationUnder the Ministry of Labour and EmploymentFormed 4 March 1952HQ New Delhi Launches ‘VISHWAS, 2026’ to Settle Damages and Penalty Disputes

  • EPFO launched VISHWAS, 2026, a one-time settlement scheme for employers.
  • It settles old disputes related to damages and penalties.
  • In effect from 29 June 2026; open for 6 months.
  • Aims: reduce litigation, encourage voluntary compliance and settle old cases faster through a fully digital, time-bound process.
  • Reduced penalty rates for defaults before 14 June 2024: up to 2 months’ delay → 0.25% per month; more than 2 months → 0.50% per month; more than 4 months → 1% per month.
⚠️ Exam trapVISHWAS is for employers (not employees). Cut-off for old defaults: 14 June 2024; scheme start: 29 June 2026.

🏦 BankingGovernment Makes TReDS Mandatory for CPSE Payments to MSMEs

  • The Ministry of MSME made it mandatory for all operating Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) to settle invoices of their MSME suppliers through RBI-authorised TReDS platforms.
  • Aim: tackle delayed payments to MSMEs and improve their access to working capital.
  • MSME invoices for goods and services supplied to CPSEs will be routed through the Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS).
  • Once an invoice is approved, the MSME can get paid before the due date by having it discounted by participating banks and NBFCs.
  • TReDS: an RBI-regulated electronic platform, operational since 2017, for MSMEs to finance receivables from corporates, government departments and PSUs.
  • Five TReDS platforms operate: RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart, C2treds and DTX.
  • Timeline (PDF chart): 2017 TReDS goes live → Nov 2018 companies with turnover above ₹500 crore and all CPSEs must onboard → Nov 2024 threshold cut to ₹250 crore → 30 June 2026 settlement through TReDS mandatory for CPSEs.
➕ Static GK: MSMED Act, 2006 — buyers must pay MSME suppliers within 45 days (Section 15) · Ministry of MSME formed 2007
⚠️ Exam trapEarlier rules only required CPSEs to register/onboard on TReDS; the 2026 rule makes settling invoices through TReDS mandatory.
